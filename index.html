About

Honors accounting student. Hands-on consultant.

I am Alexandra Titayevsky, a bilingual student at the University of Florida Fisher School of Accounting. I study accounting alongside law, communications, entrepreneurship, and business strategy — and I put that mix to work with clients and communities, not only in the classroom.

As a Senior Strategist with REM on Campus, I provide pro-bono, impact-driven consulting for small businesses in Gainesville. I also serve as a Finance Assistant at Strike Magazine and as a research assistant for Introduction to Managerial Accounting.

I am interested in work where numbers, people, and purpose meet: consulting, finance, and entrepreneurial projects that leave things better than they were found.
